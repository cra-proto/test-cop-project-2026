Personal income tax

How to file a tax return – Personal income tax

The CRA is making tax filing easier

Starting March 2027, the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) will invite 1 million eligible individuals to use a pre-filled return to file their taxes in their CRA account.

By 2029, 5.5 million individuals will get an invitation to file their 2028 taxes.

Refer to: The CRA is making tax filing easier.

You may choose to do your own taxes or have someone else do them for you using tax software or on paper. If eligible, you may be able to get your taxes done for free by invitation or at a free tax clinic.

You need to file your taxes every year to receive, or continue to receive, the benefit and credit payments you are entitled to. 

Filing due dates for 2025 taxes

  • April 30, 2026: Deadline to file your taxes
  • June 15, 2026: Deadline to file your taxes if you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed
