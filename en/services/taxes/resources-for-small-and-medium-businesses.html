CRA resources for small and medium businesses

Whether you’re starting a new business or managing an existing one, you’ll find the resources you need to stay compliant and up to date – all in one place. Find tax information, online services, and tools you need to start, operate, or grow your business.

Register and manage your business

Find the information you need to register and manage your business.

Business registration with the CRA

Register for a business number (BN) and CRA program accounts for your business.

Key tasks:

  • Find out when you need a BN and CRA program accounts

  • Register for a BN

  • Open or update CRA program accounts such as GST/HST, payroll, corporation income tax

Income tax filing for your business

Filing requirements for business income tax, including corporation income tax (T2), sole proprietor or partnership return, and other required forms.

Key tasks:

  • File a corporation income tax (T2) return, sole proprietor, or partnership return

  • Claim business expenses

  • Claim federal tax credits, rebates, and investment tax credits

  • Make tax payments including instalment payments

  • Know when late-filing penalties and interest are applied

GST/HST

Register, charge, file, and remit the goods and services tax/harmonized sales tax (GST/HST).

Key tasks:

  • Find out when to register for the GST/HST

  • Charge and collect the GST/HST

  • File a GST/HST return

  • Get a GST/HST access code

  • Remit the tax you collected

  • Claim GST/HST rebates

  • Confirm a GST/HST number

Payroll

Understand your responsibilities when you pay employees.

Key tasks:

  • Open or manage your payroll account

  • Calculate source deductions

  • Remit payroll deductions and contributions

  • Issue T4 slips and summaries

  • Calculate employment benefits, allowances, and taxable benefits

What you need to know about representatives, how to authorize them and how to cancel authorization

Key tasks:

  • Authorize a representative

  • Confirm a representative’s authorization request

Manage your business

Access your business information online in your CRA account and keep your information up to date.

Key tasks:

  • Access your business information using My Business Account in your CRA account

  • Make changes to your business information including legal name, address, ownership structure

  • Get important messages through business mail online

Excise duties and taxes, other levies and charges, and customs duties and tariffs collected by the federal government.
